# Fidelity notes

How this game reads _Infinite Jest_'s "Interdependence Day" chapter (pp. 321–342 in
the Little, Brown first edition) and endnotes 121–130, and every place it has to
decide something the novel leaves open. Where the novel and this file disagree,
the novel wins and this file gets a new row.

Offer "Pemulis's shortcut" ((r+R)/2) as a labeled option, off by default, and "Old rules: Yahtzee dice" as another. Normalize so AMNAT and SOVWAR land near 400 Mt each in the default scenario.                                                                                                                  |
| A6  | Map projection and which court holds which region.                                                                                                                                                      | Four courts with run-off side by side: 73.15 m east–west by 36.57 m north–south, a 2:1 equirectangular world map centered on Greenwich. Scale is about 548 km per court metre, so the 10.1 km Peoria radius is about 1.8 cm. |

| A11 | Numeric point values for target items are never given.                                                                                                                                                  | Start with these weights and keep them in data: MAMA = population-scaled (a 10-million city is 20); towel 3; CONFORCON 4; SSTRAC 10; armband 8 (Annular Fusion lab 15); sock 6; shoe 7.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| A12 | When the game ends.                                                                                                                                                                                     | When a winner is confirmed by EndStat after: every Combatant's bucket is empty, every Combatant but one is out of contention, all remaining Combatants agree to end it, or the scenario's clock reaches its end (1600h in the Interdependence Day scenario).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

| A16 | How "normalize to about 400 Mt" turns ratios into balls.                    | Megatons per unit of ratio are set so the mean of AMNAT's and SOVWAR's figures is exactly 400 Mt (if neither plays, the largest figure gets 400 Mt). Balls nobody is allocated stay in the supply.                                                           | M1    |
| A17 | How the old red Yahtzee dice allocated megatonnage.                         | The novel only says allocation used to be done with them. The dice are seeded, so a game replays.                                                                                                                                                            | M1    |

| A32 | Where the racquet meets the ball.                                           | At the launch platform's point on the map, 1 m above the court. | A33 | How a ball flies.                                                           | A regulation ball (57 g, 6.7 cm) in still sea-level air: gravity, quadratic drag (Cd 0.55) and Magnus lift from spin (C_L = 1/(2 + 1/S), after Cross and Lindsey's tennis-ball measurements), integrated with Runge–Kutta at 1 ms. Spin does not decay over a flight of a few seconds. Court wind is not applied yet. Nets, fences, players and buckets do not stop a ball in flight; what happens when a ball hits apparatus is M6. | M3    |
| A34 | What skill a lob takes.                                                     | The player sets bearing, elevation, racquet pace and topspin. "Aim on the map" points the racquet and sets the pace a clean strike needs, standing in for a trained player's feel. The skill left is timing: a meter sweeps from early to late and back every 1.6 s, and the swing goes off where it is stopped. At either end the ball leaves 8° off line and 12% short of pace, in proportion between.                             | M3    |
| A35 | How a MIRV jockstrap flies and opens.                                       | k balls in a jockstrap weigh k × 57 g plus 30 g of strap, present ∛k × 1.25 times a ball's width to the air (Cd 0.8), and tumble, so topspin does nothing. It opens at the top of its arc. Each warhead leaves with up to 1.5 m/s of seeded sideways push and up to 0.2 m/s up or down, then flies as a ball.                                                                                                                        | M3    |
| A36 | The howitzer in play.                                                       | It is lobbed like any ball, from where the howitzer is emplaced. An order whose declared aim is beyond 50 km is refused, and a shell that lands beyond 50 km of court distance does nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | M3    |
| A37 | Kill radius before EndStat weighs terrain.                                  | Every target uses Peoria's open-ground radius of 10.1 km, about 1.8 cm of court. M4 replaces this with EndStat's terrain, high-rise and firestorm rules.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | M3    |
| A38 | Whether distances are measured on the court or on the globe.                | Rules measure on the court, because that is where the ball is (the howitzer's reach is court distance). Distances shown to players ("about 7,820 km away") are great-circle distances on the globe.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | M3    |
| A39 | When warheads leave the bucket.                                             | At the swing, whatever they hit. Only Combatants swing; the game-master does not. One launch is one ball or one jockstrap.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | M3    |
| A40 | How terrain and weather change the kill radius.                             | Peoria's 10.1 km on open flat ground is the base for a 5 Mt ground burst. Coastal × 0.85, rolling × 0.8, mountainous × 0.55, at sea × 0.9; a high-rise skyline × 0.9; scenario rain × 0.95 and snow × 0.92 over the target's court. Court weather is never an input.                                                                                                                                                                 | M4    |
| A41 | How much a strike does.                                                     | Severity is 1 inside the garment and falls linearly to 0 at the kill radius. In a city, blast and heat kill 45% × severity² of the living and injure 30% × severity of the rest. A site loses severity of its remaining value; hardened sites (SSTRAC, missile and antimissile installations, silo clusters) only severity³ from an indirect hit. A launch platform half destroyed can no longer launch.                             | M4    |
| A42 | When a city burns.                                                          | A strike in the inner half of the kill radius counts toward a firestorm; one is enough for most cities and Toronto's skyline needs two. A firestorm kills half of those still alive.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | M4    |
| A43 | The radiation formula.                                                      | At or above 100 roentgens, deaths are 0.0636 per head per 1,000 roentgens (6.36 per hundred at 1,000 R) and everyone else exposed has a shortened life. Below 100 R nobody is counted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | M4    |
| A44 | Fallout.                                                                    | Every ground burst lays a plume downwind by the scenario wind of its court: 4,800 R ten kilometres out in a 20 km/h wind, falling with distance to the power 1.2 and spreading sideways. Scenario rain or snow brings more down within 150 km and less beyond. Fallout reaches cities, not sites, and is scored for the striker. The map shows each plume where it passes 100 R.                                                     | M4    |
| A45 | EMP and the hotline.                                                        | A detonation over a Combatant's territory costs it 6% of its communication capacity, and a hit on its command site or satellite receiver another 15%. Civil-defence spending shields ten times its share of the military budget, at most 60%. The hotline carries 80 words a message at full capacity, in proportion below.                                                                                                          | M4    |
| A46 | Points for people.                                                          | 2 points per million dead (A11's MAMA weight), and a quarter of that per million injured or with shortened lives.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | M4    |
| A47 | The four corrections.                                                       | Initial megatonnage: INDDIR × √(mean megatonnage ÷ own), so a big arsenal is expected to do more. Population density: each victim's INDDIR × (mean density ÷ victim's density)^¼, so crowded targets count for less. Land-Sea-Air: SUFDDIR × (1 − ¼ × triad spread), where spread is 1 − Σ share² scaled to 1 for three equal legs. Civil defence: SUFDDIR × (1 − 5 × civil-defence share of the military budget), at most 30% off.  | M4    |
| A48 | SACPOP and its penalty.                                                     | A strike in the inner half of the kill radius of a city belonging to another playing Combatant is SACPOP. A Combatant that goes SACPOP takes one point of SUFDDIR for every point it inflicted on cities, so an exchange of cities leaves both sides below a ratio of 1.                                                                                                                                                             | M4    |
| A49 | Own goals and Combatants not playing.                                       | A strike on one's own territory is SUFDDIR only. A strike on a Combatant not in this game is a regrettable mistargeting, like one on an unplayed nation (A13).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | M4    |
| A50 | Ratios and the winner.                                                      | Ratio is corrected INDDIR ÷ corrected SUFDDIR (with SACPOP penalties). Nothing suffered and something inflicted is an infinite ratio; a Combatant nothing has happened to has no ratio and ranks last. The winner is the best ratio, provided it has inflicted something. When the game ends is M5 (A12).                                                                                                                            | M4    |
| A51 | The three hours.                                                            | The crunch prints EndStat's working line by line. It takes four seconds unless "Authentic three hours" is ticked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | M4    |
| A52 | Who sees EndStat.                                                           | The strike log is public, as balls landing on the courts are. The game-master sees every tally; a Combatant sees only its own until the crunch, which shows all of them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | M4    |
